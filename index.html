About SMARTVEG

SMARTVEG is a vegetable waste management initiative focused on reducing food waste in Dapoli.

Our Objectives

  • Reduce vegetable wastage.
  • Improve stock management.
  • Connect vendors and hotels.
  • Estimate vegetable waste risk.
  • Promote compost and fertilizer production from vegetable waste.

Our Vision

Pre-Waste Action – Take action before vegetables become waste.
